How does the PHP community show up where developers ask for help? This section looks at PHP's share of questions on Stack Overflow over time.

<Alert status="info">
These pages show published results from the PHP Foundation SIG research (<a href="https://github.com/ThePHPF/SIG-ambassadors/pull/40">SIG-ambassadors, PR #40</a>).
They are not refreshed by this project's query runner.
</Alert>

- [Stack Overflow questions](/community/stack-overflow): PHP's share of all Stack Overflow questions, by quarter and year, since 2008

**Reading community data.** Questions asked are a measure of how developers use a help site, not of how many people write PHP.
A change in that site's behaviour changes the numbers without any change in PHP.
